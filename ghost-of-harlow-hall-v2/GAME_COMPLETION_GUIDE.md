# The Ghost of Harlow Hall v2 — Completion Guide

## Win conditions

For the best ending and certificate:

- collect all 12 evidence items
- complete all NPC interviews
- flag the ethics concern in the IT Department
- accuse **Darren Koh**
- finish with a score of **95 or higher**

## v2 flow

1. Sign in with your full name and student ID.
2. Start the case from the title screen.
3. Follow the tutorial on the first visit to Nadia's room, or replay it later from the `?` header button or the phone Help app.
4. Use the smartphone HUD to review locations, evidence, the case journal, export tools, and audio settings.
5. Finish the Security Office briefing and submit your verdict.
6. Open the print-ready case report from the ending screen or the phone Export app.
7. If eligible, open the certificate from the ending screen or the Export app.

## Certificate eligibility

A certificate is only available when all of the following are true:

- correct suspect: `Darren Koh`
- ethics concern flagged
- final score `>= 95`

## Rank mapping

- `100` → `Master Investigator`
- `95–99` → `Senior Analyst`
- `<95` → no certificate
